How it works

Four steps. No sign-up.

  1. Pick a mode

    Plants, Rocks or Coins.

  2. Snap a photo

    Fill the frame, use good light. The model runs on your phone.

  3. Read the result

    Best match, a confidence bar, and the next-best matches underneath.

  4. Keep it

    Save the find with its photo, date and notes, or share a find card.

Questions

Fair questions.

Is it always right?

No, and it won't pretend to be. Every result shows how confident the model is and the next-best matches. When confidence is low, Fieldkey says "not sure, try another angle" instead of guessing. A clear, close photo in good light helps most.

Do my photos get uploaded?

No. Identification runs on your phone. Your photos never leave the device unless you share a find card yourself.

Does it need an internet connection?

Not to identify. The models are inside the app, so it works offline, on a trail or in a field. The free version needs a connection to load ads, and purchases go through Google Play.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Your collection lives on your phone.

Can it tell me if a plant is safe to eat, or safe for my pet?

It shows general safety and toxicity information about the species it suggests. That is not medical or veterinary advice, and the identification itself can be wrong. Never eat a plant or mushroom based on an app. If a person or pet may have eaten something, contact a doctor, a vet or poison control.

How much is my coin worth?

Fieldkey reads the year and mint mark to flag silver content and well-known key dates, and shows values only as an estimated range. Condition, varieties and the market change real prices a lot, so check a dealer before you buy or sell.

Which coins does it know?

About 22 US coin types. Coins from other countries aren't included yet.

What do I get for free?

3 identifications a day. Need one more? Watch a short video ad and get another. Pro removes the limit and the ads and adds collection export.

How do I cancel Pro?

Pro is billed through Google Play. Cancel any time in the Play Store app: Profile, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions. If you cancel during the 3-day free trial of the yearly plan, you are not charged.

When can I get it?

Fieldkey is coming soon to Google Play, for Android phones.

A result was wrong. Can I tell you?

Please do, it helps. Report a wrong ID on GitHub with what it said and what it really was. Issues are public, so leave out anything personal, like your location.

How do I delete my data?

There is no account, so everything lives on your phone. Use Settings, Delete all data, or uninstall the app. Details are on the data deletion page.
