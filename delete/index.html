Delete your data

Fieldkey: Plant Rock Coin ID · Hartmann Studios · Last updated: October 15, 2026

There is no Fieldkey account. We never receive your photos, finds or notes, so there is nothing stored on our side to delete. Everything the app keeps is on your phone, and you can remove it yourself at any time.

Delete everything from inside the app

  1. Open Fieldkey.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete all data and confirm.

This removes your saved finds, their photos, your notes and your settings from your phone immediately.

Or uninstall the app

Uninstalling Fieldkey removes all of its data from your phone. You can also clear it without uninstalling: Android Settings → Apps → Fieldkey → Storage → Clear storage.

Things to know

  • Deleting data does not cancel a Pro subscription. Cancel in the Google Play Store app: Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Your Pro purchase belongs to your Google Play account, so Google Play can unlock it again if you reinstall.
  • Find cards or exports you shared or saved outside the app are your own files and stay where you put them.
  • If you opened a GitHub issue and want it removed, ask in a privacy question and we will delete it.
